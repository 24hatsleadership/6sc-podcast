## Show notes

Before a customer walks through the door, they are usually looking at the Google listing. Dan and Kate treat that Google Business Profile as the digital storefront, the front window, and walk through four mistakes that make a good business look less sure from the outside.

1. The listing that was never claimed. Nobody took ownership, so there is no phone number and no way to respond.
2. The wrong details. A move to another suite, winter hours that never got updated, or a phone line nobody answers.
3. The stale profile. Plenty of reviews, but the newest photos are a few years old and the posts have stopped.
4. Thin or quiet reviews. A short list of reviews, and a couple that never got a reply. Happy customers will usually leave one. They just never get asked.

People compare. A search nearby shows a few businesses side by side, and the one with fresh photos, accurate hours, and recent answered reviews looks like the safer choice.

Fixing it is two kinds of work. Profile management starts with a cleanup: claim or verify the profile, correct the name, address, phone, hours, and categories, and keep those details consistent. Then keep it active each month with posts, fresh photos, and replies. Review generation is a simple ask, by text or email, right after a visit. You can start with one of those and add the other later, and the profile stays yours.

This week's check takes about five minutes. Search the business name on Google Maps, on your phone, the way a customer would. Confirm the phone number, the hours, and the date of the newest photo. If one of them is off, fix it or add one new photo this week.
